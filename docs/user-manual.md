# Employee User Manual

## Introduction

Welcome to the K-Point App, a peer-recognition system that allows employees to acknowledge and appreciate their colleagues' contributions through digital points. This manual will guide you through the features available to employees.

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the application URL provided by your administrator
2. Enter your email address and password
3. If this is your first login or after a password reset, you will be prompted to create a new password

### Dashboard

The dashboard is your main interface and displays:

- Your current K-Point balance
- A button to give K-Points to colleagues
- Recent transactions (both sent and received)
- Notifications for new K-Points received

## Giving K-Points

1. From the dashboard, click the "Give K-Points" button
2. Select a recipient from the dropdown menu (you can search by name or filter by department)
3. The amount field will be pre-filled with the fixed amount (typically 3 points)
4. Enter a reason for giving the points (10-200 characters required)
5. Click "Submit" to complete the transaction

### Daily Limits

- You can make a limited number of transfers per day (typically 3)
- Each transfer is a fixed amount (typically 3 points)
- Your remaining daily allowance is displayed after each transaction

## Viewing Transaction History

1. Click on "Transaction History" in the navigation menu
2. View a complete list of your transactions (both sent and received)
3. Use filters to search by:
   - Transaction type (sent/received)
   - Date range
   - Keywords in the reason field
4. Export your transaction history to CSV by clicking the "Export" button

## Notifications

- You will receive in-app notifications when someone gives you K-Points
- Click the bell icon in the top navigation bar to view all notifications
- Email notifications may also be sent depending on your settings

## Quarterly Reset

At the end of each fiscal quarter (typically Mar 31, Jun 30, Sep 30, Dec 31):

- All balances are reset
- A new allocation of points (typically 20) is distributed to each active employee
- If you join mid-quarter, you will receive a prorated amount based on your start date

## Profile Settings

1. Access your profile settings from the navigation menu
2. Update your notification preferences
3. Change your password

## Need Help?

If you encounter any issues or have questions about using the application, please contact your administrator.
